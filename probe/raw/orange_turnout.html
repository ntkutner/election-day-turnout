voter turnout

Track unofficial voter turnout for the 2026 General Election. View ballots cast by voting method, party, precinct, date and voting location.

Election Night Results Begin at 7:00 PM

After the polls close at 7:00 PM on General Election Day, November 3, the Voter Turnout tool will be disabled. For the latest election results, please visit our Election Results tools which can be found on our homepage.

Having Trouble Viewing?

Click here to open the Voter Turnout tool in a new window.

This secure Voter Turnout tool is provided in partnership with our official elections software vendor, VR Systems, and is an authorized service of the Orange County Supervisor of Elections.
