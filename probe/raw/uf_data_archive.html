Data Archive

Through the links below, you will have access to election results, turnout rates, and election early voting statistics. National and state data can be accessed through the “All States (National)” link below, and precinct-level data can be accessed through individual state pages.

To access most datasets, you must first login to your free Election Science Lab account. This account provides access to most datasets in our archive. The 2022 and 2024 state precinct maps and statewide election results are available only to paid subscribers. Contact us at election-lab@ufl.edu for subscription information.
