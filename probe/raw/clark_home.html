Electronic Sample Ballots

You may receive an electronic sample ballot by e-mail instead of a paper one sent through the post office. This helps save taxpayer dollars by reducing printing, paper, and mailing costs. Log in to "Registered Voter Services" and click on "Request my Sample Ballot Electronically (Go Green)" from the dropdown menu. You will receive an e-mail confirmation. NRS 293.558 requires that the e-mail address of anyone who requests an electronic sample ballot be kept CONFIDENTIAL and may not be given to third parties. Contact us st (702) 455-VOTE (8683) or ELinfo@clarkcountynv.gov if you have any questions.
