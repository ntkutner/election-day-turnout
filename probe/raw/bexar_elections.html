  1. Latest News

    Stay Up To Date

  1. Now Available: Wait Times Map

    Bexar County voters can new check the wait times at voting locations and find the shortest wait time near them. Explore the Wait Times application.

  2. Voter Suspense List

    If your voter registration is marked as "suspense," it just means your address may need to be updated or confirmed. You can still vote. Learn more about the suspense status.

  3. Welcome to our New Website

    Your Bexar County Elections website has received a new look and a .gov domain! Election information and resources are now easier to find while still maintaining a mobile friendly and accessible site!

  4. Ballot By Mail Application

    If you received a Texas Ballot by Mail application, please check the identification number section. Some applications only have 4 spaces, but 8 digits are required. Please use the correct application before submitting. Questions? Contact our office.

  5. Voter Registration Cards

    We are aware of the issue with the voter registration cards, including the truncated last names. Our team is working on issuing corrected voter registration cards to those affected. Once the updated cards are mailed, we will notify the public. Read on.

  6. Important Postmark Change

    Bexar County residents who mail vote-by-mail ballots or other deadline sensitive items including property tax payments should be aware of an important change in how the United States Postal Service (USPS) applies postmarks. Learn more about the change.
